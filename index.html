Bridging Business Operations & Technology

I specialize in transforming manual and repetitive business processes into practical automated systems. With approximately five years of hands-on experience in Google Sheets Automation, Google Apps Script, and Web App Solutions, my approach is strictly problem-first rather than technology-first.

When working with a business, I focus on understanding how the process currently operates, identifying bottlenecks and repetitive tasks, and designing automation workflows that make daily operations faster, cleaner, and more reliable.

One of my core strengths is creative problem solving. I do not simply implement what is requested on the surface; I analyze the broader workflow to discover additional automation opportunities and connect separate processes into an integrated, efficient system.

Currently working as an independent specialist while having significant experience collaborating within company and team environments, I am also continuously expanding my capabilities into n8n and advanced AI automation.
